Manage your Discord sessions quietly & locally

Save, organize, verify and switch between Discord session tokens right from your toolbar. Everything stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Download for Chrome How to install

Why it's useful

A small toolbar popup that gives you full control over saved sessions without leaving Discord workflows.

🗂️

Personal vault

Group, favorite, tag and search your saved sessions. Add manually or from the active session.

🚀

One-click switch

Apply a saved session to a discord.com tab and reload — fast account rotation.

✅

Verify tokens

Check individual or all saved tokens right from the popup.

💾

Copy & export

Copy a token in one click, or export a JSON backup of everything.

👤

Account details

See user ID, status, joined date, badges and notes for each session.

🛡️

100% local

Data lives in browser storage only. No accounts, no telemetry, no servers.
